Introduction 
SFIT- IT department                     LUMEN                                 
‹#›
Mental health challenges are becoming increasingly common worldwide, while social stigma, lack of awareness, high treatment costs and limited accessibility continue to prevent many individuals from seeking timely professional support.
The growing use of AI-powered conversational systems offers an opportunity to provide accessible and continuous emotional support; however, general-purpose AI chatbots may miss crisis-related signals, overlook gradual negative mood trends and generate diagnosis-like or potentially inappropriate responses.
Existing conversational systems often lack long-term contextual understanding, explainable risk detection and structured escalation mechanisms, making it difficult to safely identify users who may require additional or professional support.
Lumen: a Safety-Guarded Conversational AI Architecture that integrates real-time risk classification, retrieval-augmented contextual memory, anti-diagnosis guardrails, longitudinal mood tracking, explainable escalation and human-reviewed continuous improvement to enable supportive, context-aware and non-diagnostic AI interactions.

Problem Definition 
Mental health support remains difficult to access due to social stigma, lack of awareness, high treatment costs, and limited accessibility. As a result, many individuals may rely on general-purpose AI chatbots that are not specifically designed for safe mental-health support.
Existing conversational AI systems may miss immediate crisis signals and gradual negative mood trends, generate diagnosis-like responses, and lack long-term contextual memory, explainable risk detection, and reliable escalation mechanisms for users who may require professional support.
Proposed Solution
Lumen is a Safety-Guarded Conversational AI Architecture designed to provide supportive, context-aware, and strictly non-diagnostic mental-health interactions.
Lumen combines:
Real-time crisis-risk classification to identify potential high-risk messages.
Emotion and stress detection to understand the user’s current emotional state.
Retrieval-Augmented Context Memory (RAG) to retrieve relevant information from previous conversations and provide contextual responses.
Longitudinal mood tracking to identify gradual changes in emotional state over time.
Anti-diagnosis guardrails to prevent the system from producing diagnosis-like or clinically inappropriate responses.
Structured escalation to verified professional-support resources such as Tele-MANAS when elevated risk is detected.

=== SLIDE 5 ===
Scope of project
SFIT- IT department                     LUMEN                                 
‹#›
To develop Lumen, a safety-guarded conversational AI system for supportive and preventive mental-health interactions by integrating real-time crisis-risk classification, emotion and stress detection, Retrieval-Augmented Context Memory, and longitudinal mood tracking across user conversations.
To implement a rule-based safety layer, explainable risk aggregation, anti-diagnosis guardrails, and structured escalation to verified resources such as Tele-MANAS. The system will also maintain secure audit logs and support a human-reviewed continuous improvement loop for refining risk lexicons, detection thresholds, and safety guardrails.
The system is designed to provide supportive, context-aware, and strictly non-diagnostic assistance. It is not intended to diagnose mental-health conditions or replace psychiatrists, psychologists, therapists, emergency services, or other professional mental-health care.

Hardware & software requirements
SFIT- IT department                     Lumen                                
‹#›
Hardware Requirements
Processor: Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or higher
RAM: Minimum 8 GB; 16 GB recommended
Storage: Minimum 50 GB free SSD space
GPU: Optional; NVIDIA GPU with 6 GB+ VRAM recommended for local AI model inference
Internet: Stable internet connection for cloud-based LLM and API services

Software Requirements

Operating System: Windows 10/11 or Linux
Frontend: React.js / Next.js,Tailwind CSS
Backend: Python, Flask / FastAPI
Database: PostgreSQL with pgvector
Voice Interaction: Web Speech API
AI / ML: DistilBERT / RoBERTa, Sentence Transformers, Hugging Face Transformers, PyTorch, LLM API / Ollama
Security: JWT Authentication, HTTPS/TLS Encryption, Password Hashing, Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Secure Audit Logging
Development Tools: Visual Studio Code, Git, GitHub, Postman, Node.js & npm, Python 3.x
