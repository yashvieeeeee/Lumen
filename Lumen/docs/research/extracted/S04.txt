=== SLIDE 3 ===
Introduction 
SFIT- IT department                     Lumen                           
‹#›
Mental health challenges are becoming increasingly common worldwide, while social stigma, lack of awareness, high treatment costs and limited accessibility continue to prevent many individuals from seeking timely professional support.
The growing use of AI-powered conversational systems offers an opportunity to provide accessible and continuous emotional support; however, general-purpose AI chatbots may miss crisis-related signals, overlook gradual negative mood trends and generate diagnosis-like or potentially inappropriate responses.
Existing conversational systems often lack long-term contextual understanding, explainable risk detection and structured escalation mechanisms, making it difficult to safely identify users who may require additional or professional support.
Lumen: a Safety-Guarded Conversational AI Architecture that integrates real-time risk classification, retrieval-augmented contextual memory, anti-diagnosis guardrails, longitudinal mood tracking, explainable escalation and human-reviewed continuous improvement to enable supportive, context-aware and non-diagnostic AI interactions

Problem definition & Proposed solution 
SFIT- IT department                     Project Title                                  
‹#›
Problem Definition
Mental health support remains difficult to access due to social stigma, lack of awareness, high treatment costs and limited accessibility, leading many individuals to rely on general-purpose AI chatbots that are not specifically designed for safe mental-health support.
Existing conversational AI systems may miss immediate crisis signals and gradual negative mood trends, generate diagnosis-like responses, and lack long-term contextual memory, explainable risk detection and reliable escalation mechanisms for users who may require professional support.
Proposed Solution
Lumen: a Safety-Guarded Conversational AI Architecture that combines real-time crisis-risk classification, Retrieval-Augmented Context Memory and longitudinal mood tracking to provide supportive, context-aware and strictly non-diagnostic AI interactions.
Lumen integrates anti-diagnosis guardrails, explainable risk aggregation and structured escalation to verified resources such as Tele-MANAS, supported by a human-reviewed continuous learning loop that improves risk lexicons, detection thresholds and safety guardrails over time.

=== SLIDE 7 ===
Scope of project
SFIT- IT department                     Project Title                                  
‹#›
Develop a safety-guarded conversational AI system for supportive mental-health interactions by integrating real-time crisis-risk classification, Retrieval-Augmented Context Memory, multilingual risk detection, and longitudinal mood tracking across user conversations and journal entries. 
Implement anti-diagnosis guardrails, explainable risk aggregation, structured escalation to verified resources such as Tele-MANAS, and secure audit logging with human-reviewed continuous improvement to enhance risk detection, safety guardrails, and transparency.
The system is designed to provide supportive and preventive assistance only and remains strictly non-diagnostic; it is not intended to replace psychiatrists, psychologists, therapists, emergency services, or other professional mental-health care.

Hardware Requirements
